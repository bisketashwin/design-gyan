# SORRY, BUT AI CAN’T GUARANTEE SUCCESS
## AI does not know what is critical for your game

> **What is a game?** A game is a incomplete audio visual illusion.
> **Player completes the illusion** by imagination.

* **AI CAN** Simulate balance and calculate economy curves instantly.
* **HUMANS CAN** Add frictions to that aconomy and make it fun challenge for player.
* **AI CAN** Render high-density visual polished assets in seconds.
* **HUMANS CAN** Make a asset uninq item only found in this game with subtext, or emotional pacing.
* **AI CAN** Generate code fast, automate pipelines, and draft shader variants.
* **HUMANS CAN** Prevent over-engineering or architectural code bloat.

# HO DO HUMANS DO IT? 
## DESIGN IS INTENTIONALITY

> *The Stronger the Intent, the More Unique the Design*
> It can't be done in a room or in AI. You need real-world inputs.

* **EVERY TIME YOU START A NEW GAME IDEA:**
* 1. Go to a local gaming cafe.
* 2. Buy a 1-hour pass for a gamer and just watch them play.
* 3. For the next hour, play the game yourself.
* 4. Talk to at least 5 random players post-game.
* *Rule:* Do NOT talk to your friends or friends of the gamers.

---

# WHAT TO ASK THE PLAYERS
## Extracting honest opinions

> **The 5 Whys Rule:** Ask why they liked or disliked, *ask why* 5 times.

* Observe and listen for mentions of: Plot & Story, Levels & Difficulty, Rewards, Achievements & Quests, Leaderboards.
* Player didn't mention art, animation, or sound? **Ask them directly.**
* Drill down past surface reactions to get to the core psychological trigger.

# THE 3-MONTH and 3 PROTOTYPE ITERATION RULE
## Rough build in 3 weeks, playtest, iterate for another 3 weeks 

> Rough visual and animations, buggy logic, key game flows, polish in Iterations. 

* **Core game loop** Gamers should have fun find it challanging wanting to play more.
* **Rough art and animation** Placeholder assets or AI genarated assets will do.
* **Buggy code** Players dont notice bugs unlerss they play it 10 times.
* **The Reality Check:** Ask 5 whys to haters and likers.
